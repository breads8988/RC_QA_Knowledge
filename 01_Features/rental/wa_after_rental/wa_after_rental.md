# After Rental — Web App (`ARE`)

The post-rental vehicle-condition flow: from the "Car rental cost estimates" page, the driver captures photos of any new damage after returning a rental vehicle, reviews the AI-estimated repair cost, then views a side-by-side comparison against their Before Rental (`BRE`) submission.
